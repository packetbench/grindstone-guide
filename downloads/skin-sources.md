# Wrestling skin health handout sources and photo credits

Prepared September 26, 2026 for Grindstone Wrestling families. This companion explains the sources for the one-page visual reference and its contact steps. The photographs help parents recognize a concern to discuss with a healthcare professional and coach. They cannot diagnose a child's rash or clear a wrestler to participate. The handout gives no treatment instructions.

## Guidance sources

- [USA Wrestling current rulebook](https://www.usawmembership.com/usa_wrestling_rule_book), Appendix A, Title 2: medical checks and infectious-lesion eligibility at applicable USA Wrestling events. Those event rules are not automatically MMWL policy.
- [CDC ringworm basics](https://www.cdc.gov/ringworm/about/): fungal infection, typical scaly rash, color variation and the need for assessment when conditions look similar.
- [American Academy of Dermatology ringworm symptoms](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/a-z/ringworm-symptoms): skin and scalp signs, including scaling and hair loss.
- [CDC impetigo](https://www.cdc.gov/group-a-strep/about/impetigo.html): leaking sores and yellow or honey-colored crusts. Its school/daycare return guidance is not a wrestling return rule.
- [CDC athlete MRSA prevention and control](https://www.cdc.gov/mrsa/prevention/athletes.html): painful, warm, swollen or draining sores; professional assessment and reporting. A visual inspection cannot reliably distinguish MRSA from other bacteria.
- [CDC staph basics](https://www.cdc.gov/staphylococcus-aureus/about/): skin infections can resemble pimples or boils.
- [NFHS wrestling skin-lesion form](https://www.nfhs.org/resources/sports/nfhs-wrestling-skin-lesion-form): assessment and documentation framework. Ask the coach which form and return rules the actual league or event requires.

## Return to wrestling

The handout directs parents to ask a healthcare professional what the condition is and whether a cream or another form of care is appropriate. It does not recommend a medicine, a treatment duration or a fixed return date. Parents should ask the coach what documentation and return process the actual league or event requires. USA Wrestling or NFHS national guidance does not establish a new MMWL policy.
